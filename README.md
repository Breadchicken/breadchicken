## 🚀 About Me

Hey! I'm a **Fullstack Developer** from **Germany** 🇩🇪 and the founder of **[Novastrix](https://novastrix.com/)** — a consulting brand for **Shopware 6 solutions** and **n8n automations** built for online merchants.

I'm also working with **[@minaxum-it](https://github.com/minaxum-it)**. As an active online merchant myself, I build what I use every day in my own shops — practical, hands-on, with a clear focus on ROI.

> *"Automatisierung die sich rechnet. Produktdaten die überzeugen."*

💡 I especially love working with modern tech stacks and automating workflows - there's always something new to learn!
